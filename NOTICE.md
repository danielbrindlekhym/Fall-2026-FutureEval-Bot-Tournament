This project follows the entry workflow and forecasting principles documented by Metaculus:

- https://www.metaculus.com/futureeval/
- https://github.com/Metaculus/metac-bot-template (reviewed commit da5de87fcfefc324af9aa268cbc4efdd6b9fbfea)
